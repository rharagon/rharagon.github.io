About

I am a researcher in the SoftDev Research Group at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Rey Juan Carlos University (Madrid, Spain).

My work focuses on the intersection of Science of Learning, Software Engineering, and Artificial Intelligence, with particular attention to:

  • Automated assessment of programming skills
  • Data science in educational settings
  • Software maintenance and evolution
  • Open-source project analysis

I am currently pursuing my PhD. You can find my full profile on ORCID.

Publications

Registered Report
How Do Code Smells Affect Skill Growth in Scratch Novice Programmers?
Registered Report · ICSME 2025 (International Conference on Software Maintenance)
Large-scale study of ~2M Scratch projects establishing the first correlation between computational thinking skillsand design-level code smells. Results link novice programming habits to concrete design flaws, informingcurricula and automated feedback for sustainable software maintenance.
SSRN: 5363087
Registered Report
Can we Disentangle Size from Quality to Better Understand Software Maintenance & Evolution? A Study with 2M Scratch Projects
Registered Report · ICSME 2026 (International Conference on Software Maintenance)
A large-scale empirical study with 2 million Scratch projects to separate the effects of size and quality in software maintenance research.
SSRN: 6984618
Conference Paper
A CEFR-Inspired Classification Framework with Fuzzy C-Means to Automate Assessment of Programming Skills in Scratch
CSEDU 2026 — Proceedings of the 18th International Conference on Computer Supported Education
Proposes a CEFR-inspired classification framework using Fuzzy C-Means clustering to automate the assessment of programming skills in Scratch, inspired by the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages.
DOI: 10.5220/0014691000004021
